Behind The Scenes News & Updates

Hi, I'm Seth! I'm a small town boy whose favorite activities include making dice, playing Dungeons & Dragons, playing video games, and annoying my family. When I'm not doing any of those, I'm usually watching The Good Place, Adventure Time, or whatever horror film I can find.

Making resin dice for TTRPGs started out as my hobby, specifically dice for Dungeons & Dragons 5e & 5.5e. Now I want to turn that hobby into a business, and Twelve Caps is how I'm doing it. I'm still figuring out the ropes of resin casting, but I'm already designing unique and stylized dice.

I'm a friendly fellow, looking to make exciting dice for players around the world. Hopefully I get to see you around!
