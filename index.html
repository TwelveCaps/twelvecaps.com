Welcome to Twelve Caps

My name is Seth, and I am a small town boy who loves making dice, playing Dungeons & Dragons, playing video games, and annoying my family. I also enjoy watching The Good Place, Adventure Time, and a wide variety of horror films.

Dice making began as my hobby, and I am now working to turn it into a business. I cast resin dice for tabletop role-playing games, with a focus on Dungeons & Dragons 5e and 5.5e. I am still learning the craft of resin casting, and I am already designing dice with a unique and stylized look.

I am a friendly fellow, and my goal is to make exciting dice for players around the world. I hope to see you around.
