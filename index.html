Introduction


What does Twelve Caps do?
Twelve Caps focuses mainly on two things.
Producing and selling resin dice & developing small indie Roblox video games.
Whilst trying to keep our games free to play & our dice at reasonable prices.

Why support Twelve Caps?
All of the content produced by Twelve Caps is 100% human made.
As my budget grows, I intend to add increasingly unique dice to my store.
If you're interested in video games:
I have a small cozy social game I'm working on the side, that will be free to play.

Who created Twelve Caps and why?
I. Seth, a small town boy from the US, started making dice as a hobby.
But as it expanded, I found a way to monitize it through Etsy.
Trying to bring awesome and inspiring dice to your game tables.
